Meet the designer

Hi, I'm Merit

I'm Ruth Mmachi Oragui, but you can call me Merit. I'm the creative force behind Merit Rose Design, and I love creating dresses that make women feel amazing.

For me, fashion isn't just about clothes. It's about expressing yourself, celebrating your individuality and turning everyday moments into something special. I'm drawn to bold colours, unexpected details and designs as unique as the women who wear them.

Every piece is cut and finished with care, so it fits well and looks exactly the way you imagined. Let's create something beautiful together.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order a custom dress?

Send us a message on WhatsApp with your style idea or a picture, your event date and your measurements. We will confirm the design, fabric and price before we start.

Can I bring my own fabric?

Yes. You can supply your own fabric or ask us to source fabric for you.

Do you make bridal and wedding guest outfits?

Yes. We design bridal gowns, reception dresses, bridesmaid and aso-ebi styles, and owambe outfits.

How long does a custom dress take?

It depends on the design and our current bookings. Tell us your event date when you enquire and we will confirm a delivery date.
